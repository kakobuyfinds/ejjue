The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping research worksheet used to organize product links, categories, variants, prices, and quality-check notes. Its usefulness depends on accurate entries and working source links. A clear sheet helps you compare options before deciding what to buy.

02 / The research routine

How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet.

A small, well-kept shortlist is easier to evaluate. Give every entry a source, a reason, and a date.

Find your next starting point ↗

  1. Choose a category

    Begin with the item you need: shoes, a layer, headwear, or an accessory. Keep different product types in separate groups.

  2. Record the exact listing

    Save the source URL, seller reference, color, size, and listed price with its currency. Add the date you checked it.

  3. Compare the evidence

    Read measurements and material information. Review available QC photos for visible details and consistency with the selected variant.

  4. Estimate the complete cost

    Account for applicable shipping, service fees, taxes, and duties. Recheck the final checkout information before committing.

05 / Answers before checkout

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Direct answers about spreadsheet research, product evidence, sizing, and costs.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping research worksheet used to organize product links, categories, variants, prices, and quality-check notes. Its usefulness depends on accurate entries and working source links.

How do I use a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open the source listing, record the exact variant and size, compare measurements and available photos, and estimate the complete cost before making a purchase decision.

What should a useful product spreadsheet contain?

Include the product title, source URL, category, seller reference, variant, size measurements, listed price and currency, shipping estimate, quality-check notes, and the date you last checked the listing.

Does a spreadsheet entry prove product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, or product photograph does not prove authenticity. Check the seller’s evidence, product documentation, and applicable purchase protections.

How should I compare prices across listings?

Compare the same variant in the same currency. Add applicable domestic shipping, service fees, international delivery, taxes, and duties, and keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide that links to CAFANS. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kakobuy or the brands shown.
